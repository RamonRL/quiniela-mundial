## Business description (corta · ~250 chars)

> Quiniela Mundial 2026 is a private skill-based football prediction game for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Users predict match scores, group standings and scorers for the 104 matches, earning points by accuracy. No entry fee, no monetary prizes, no chance-based outcomes.

## Business description (larga · si Paddle pide más detalle)

> **What it is**
> A web/PWA platform that hosts private prediction pools for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Each user creates or joins a "pool" (free up to 20 members; paid plans for larger groups up to 250) and submits predictions across six categories:
>
> 1. Group standings (1st to 4th in each of the 12 groups)
> 2. Knockout bracket (R32 → final)
> 3. Tournament top scorer
> 4. Special predictions (best goalkeeper, host country's best result, etc.)
> 5. Per-match score predictions for all 104 matches
> 6. Per-match scorer predictions
>
> Points are awarded automatically as real match results come in, based on a transparent rules table (e.g. exact-score match = +5 points, correct group winner = +6 points). The user with the most points at the end of the tournament wins the pool.
>
> **What it is NOT**
> - **Not gambling**: there is no entry fee paid to join a pool. The free tier allows up to 20 members at €0. Larger paid plans (€19–€99) cover server capacity for groups of 50/100/250 members, not pool entry.
> - **Not chance-based**: outcomes depend entirely on user skill in predicting real-world football results.
> - **No monetary prizes**: the platform does not award money. The winner of each pool receives recognition among friends; for paid corporate plans, the winning company receives a physical trophy and social-media coverage. No cash payout, ever.
> - **Not a sweepstakes/lottery**: there are no random drawings or chance mechanics.
>
> **Legal positioning** (Spain, where the operator is based)
> The service is explicitly excluded from the scope of Ley 13/2011 de regulación del juego (Spanish Gambling Act) because (a) there is no monetary contraprestación to participate and (b) outcomes are not chance-based. This is stated in the Terms of Service at /terminos.

## Why "Quiniela" appears in the brand name

> "Quiniela" in Spanish colloquially means "prediction pool" — the same word is used for office World Cup pools, family pools, friend pools. It does **not** imply gambling or money. The brand is a wordplay on the cultural concept of friends predicting World Cup results together. The product itself has zero monetary stakes for participants.
